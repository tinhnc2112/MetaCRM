# MetaCRM FINAL v2 — Executive Specification
Baseline repository: `650504054d2037bf78c5d2e22082c83aad829dc2`

## Product
Internal desktop-first shop app.
- Facebook Page first channel
- Messenger selling
- Facebook comments
- COD
- One warehouse
- J&T shipping
- Staff chat/care/order
- Admin products/inventory/staff/reports
- No SaaS commercialization

## Product principles
1. Fast daily operation.
2. Customer is channel-independent.
3. One Order domain for Facebook and POS.
4. Inventory is transactional and authoritative.
5. Shipping providers are adapters.
6. Existing working CRM is incrementally refactored.
7. Backend owns money, stock and authorization.
8. External events are idempotent.
9. Future channels can plug into the same core.

## Non-goals
No multi-tenant SaaS, subscription billing, accounting suite, manufacturing, complex product matrix, multiple warehouses in MVP, AI autonomous sales, or payment gateway marketplace.
