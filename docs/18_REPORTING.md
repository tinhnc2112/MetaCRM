## Definitions
Recognized/collected revenue = collected COD payment tied to delivered/confirmed-collected orders.

COGS = quantity × cost_price_snapshot for recognized sales.

Gross profit = recognized revenue - COGS.

Never calculate historical COGS using today's product cost.

Admin-only COGS/profit.
